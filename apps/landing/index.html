How a shop day runs in the app

The shop works Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. This is what each person does in Lightning Shop along the way.

  1. 8:00 AM
    Shop Manager

    Know what needs you first

    The dashboard puts four counts on top (Blocked or paused, Ready to close, Ready to Go, Unassigned) and a Needs you list in the order to act on it.

  2. 8:15 AM
    Shop Manager

    See the whole yard

    Yard Operations shows every truck, the step it is in and what is stopping it. Filter by blocked, leaving soon or out of the shop.

  3. 8:20 AM
    Shop Manager

    Open a Work Order in one step

    Pick the truck, set the priority, add a note. Add Jobs from the Job Catalog and assign them to a mechanic or a team.

  4. 8:30 AM
    Mechanic

    Work from the phone

    Each mechanic sees their own Jobs, starts and stops the clock, takes Jobs from the team queue and leaves notes. A Job that is not in the catalog goes to the Shop Manager to review.

  5. 10:00 AM
    Mechanic → Shop Manager

    Report a blocker in seconds

    Waiting for parts or for approval? The mechanic says why, the truck shows up as blocked on the dashboard, and the Shop Manager resolves it in one tap.

  6. 11:00 AM
    PM Mechanic

    Preventive maintenance with mileage

    PM has its own screen: record the mileage and the parts used, so the next service is based on real numbers.

  7. 1:00 PM
    Safety

    Nothing leaves without Quality Check

    Safety takes the finished truck, approves it, or sends the Job back to the mechanic with a note.

  8. 2:30 PM
    Shop Manager

    Ready to Go, then Return to Service

    When inspection, PM, alignment, detailing and Quality Check are done, the Work Order closes and the truck is Ready to Go until it leaves the shop.

  9. 4:00 PM
    Shop Manager

    Know what every truck costs

    Parts & costs keeps purchases, outside repairs (with the vendor's invoice attached) and labor hours at the shop rate, per truck and per Work Order.

  10. 4:45 PM
    Shop Manager

    Generate the invoices for Finance

    Month close lists every truck with costs to bill and drafts one internal invoice per truck, numbered for you (SHOP-2026-0001). Download the PDF and send it to Finance. Costs not billed carry over to the next month.

  11. 5:00 PM
    Automatic

    The day closes on its own

    At closing time the app stops any clock still running, so hours are never left open overnight. Mechanics tap Continue the next morning.

Who uses it

Five roles, each with the screens they need. In English or Spanish, whichever each person picks.

Shop Manager

Computer

Opens and assigns the work, resolves blockers, closes Work Orders, keeps Parts & costs and closes the month for Finance.

Mechanic

Phone

Works their Jobs with a clock, takes Jobs from the team queue, leaves notes and reports blockers. Some mechanics also own a step, like alignment or detailing.

PM Mechanic

Phone

Does preventive maintenance and records mileage and parts.

Safety

Phone

Runs Quality Check: approves the truck or sends the Job back with a note.

System Admin

Computer

Everything the Shop Manager does, plus people, the Job Catalog, the team for each step and the shop hours.

How to get into the demo

A guided day at the shop in 15 short chapters, about 20 minutes. Stop whenever you want.

  1. Open the demo

    Use the button below. It works on a computer or a phone; a computer shows more at once.

  2. Type your access code

    You received it with this link. Type it and select Open the demo. If you don't have it, ask the person who sent you the link.

  3. Select Start the demo

    You start as the Shop Manager. Each step dims the screen and highlights what to tap, then Next takes you on.

  4. Switch roles

    Open the Demo panel to become a Mechanic, the PM Mechanic, Safety or the System Admin and see the same truck from their side.

The trucks and people are made up, and everyone with the link shares them. The data resets every night, so try anything.
