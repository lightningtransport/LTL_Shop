Everything the shop does, in one place

Built with the shop floor, for the questions people ask every day: what needs me now, where is every truck, can it leave, what did it cost.

Dashboard: what needs you now

Four counters and a "Needs you" queue: blocked or paused, ready to close, ready to go, unassigned. Open it and you know what to do next.

Yard board: where every truck is

A live board from arrival to ready to go. Filter by blocked, leaving soon or out of shop, and move trucks with a drag.

Work orders in one step

Truck, priority, a note, and the work is open. Add jobs from the catalog, assign them, pause and close.

The mechanic's phone

My jobs, a clock that starts and stops the work, the team queue, notes, and a blocker reported in two taps.

Preventive maintenance

PM with mileage and the parts used, on its own lane so it never gets lost behind repairs.

Quality check

Safety takes the truck, approves it or sends it back with a note. Nothing leaves without a check.

The truck: can it leave?

One page per truck with what is stopping it, its history and what it has cost, job by job.

Parts & costs

Purchases, outside repairs, vendors and the labor rate, each tied to the truck and the work order it belongs to.

Month close

Every truck with costs to bill, drafts made in one click, internal invoices sent to Finance as a PDF.

People and shop hours

Add staff with a temporary password, set the team for each step and the hours the day closes on its own.

Custom jobs inbox

When a mechanic writes a job that isn't in the catalog, it lands here to link, add or leave as is.

How the demo works

A guided day at a truck shop. It takes about 20 minutes start to finish, and you can stop anywhere.

  1. 1

    Open the demo

    Use the button below. It works on a computer or a phone; a computer shows more at once.

  2. 2

    Type your access code

    The code came to you together with this link. Type it once and press Open the demo. Don't have one? Ask the person who sent you the link.

  3. 3

    Press "Start the demo"

    You start as the shop manager. A guide darkens the screen and lights up what to tap, step by step, across 15 chapters: from the first truck in to the month close.

  4. 4

    Switch roles anytime

    From the Demo panel, become the mechanic, PM, quality check or admin and see the same truck from their side.

The data is made up and everyone shares it. It resets every night, so feel free to try anything.
