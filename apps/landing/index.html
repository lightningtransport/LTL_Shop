The day

How a shop day runs in the app

The shop works Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. This is who does what along the way.

  1. 8:00 AM
    Shop Manager

    Checks the dashboard

    Blocked, ready to close, ready to go and unassigned, in order.

  2. 8:20 AM
    Shop Manager

    Opens a Work Order

    Truck, priority and a note. Jobs from the Job Catalog, assigned to a mechanic or a team.

  3. 8:30 AM
    Mechanic

    Starts the clock on a Job

    From the phone. A Job that is not in the catalog goes to the Shop Manager to review.

  4. 10:00 AM
    Mechanic → Shop Manager

    Reports a blocker

    Waiting for parts or approval. The Shop Manager resolves it in one tap.

  5. 11:00 AM
    PM Mechanic

    Does preventive maintenance

    Records the mileage and the parts used.

  6. 1:00 PM
    Safety

    Runs Quality Check

    Approves the truck or sends the Job back with a note.

  7. 2:30 PM
    Shop Manager

    Closes the Work Order

    The truck is Ready to Go until Return to Service.

  8. 4:45 PM
    Shop Manager

    Closes the month for Finance

    One internal invoice per truck. Costs not billed carry over.

  9. 5:00 PM
    Automatic

    Day close

    Any clock still running stops.

Roles

Five roles, one shop

Each person sees the screens they need, in English or Spanish.

SMComputer

Shop Manager

Opens and assigns the work, resolves blockers, closes Work Orders, keeps Parts & costs and closes the month.

MPhone

Mechanic

Works their Jobs with a clock, takes Jobs from the team queue and reports blockers. Some own a step, like alignment or detailing.

PMPhone

PM Mechanic

Does preventive maintenance and records mileage and parts.

SPhone

Safety

Runs Quality Check: approves the truck or sends the Job back with a note.

SAComputer

System Admin

Everything the Shop Manager does, plus people, the Job Catalog, the team for each step and shop hours.

FAQ

Before you start

Where do I get the access code?

It comes with the link, from the person who sent it to you. If you don't have it, ask them.

Is the data real?

No. The trucks, people and costs are made up. Everyone with the link shares the same data, and it resets every night.

Can I break something?

No. Try anything: open, block, approve, close. The next morning the demo starts clean.

Do I need to create an account?

No. With the access code you go straight in, and you switch between roles from the Demo panel.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Mechanics and Safety use the app on their phone every day. The Shop Manager screens are easier on a computer.
